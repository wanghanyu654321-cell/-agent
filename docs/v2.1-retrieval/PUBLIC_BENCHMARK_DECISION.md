# V2.1 Public Real-World Knowledge Benchmark Decision

On 2026-08-29 the V2.1 pilot changed by explicit product decision from a private store corpus to a bounded public benchmark. `approved` now means approved as factual evidence in this benchmark after review of the cited public official source; it never means that any particular beauty store adopted the policy.

The corpus is restricted to public official Meituan and Dianping material. Public complaint material supplies only de-identified, paraphrased query scenarios; it never supplies a benchmark fact. Synthetic queries are explicitly test augmentation. The benchmark validates public local-services support retrieval, not a store's production policy or customer data.
