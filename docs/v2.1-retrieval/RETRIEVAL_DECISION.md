# V2.1 Retrieval Decision

`DETERMINISTIC LEXICAL RETRIEVAL SUFFICIENT FOR CURRENT PUBLIC PILOT`

The public benchmark's FINAL result is Top-1 Hit Rate 96%, Recall@3 100%, no-answer rejection 100%, wrong-evidence rate 0%, and zero tenant/store or unauthorized-evidence exposure. The real runtime and persisted audit evaluation also reports zero unsupported business facts and full evidence/version tracing.

There is therefore no evidence in the current public benchmark to add embeddings, a vector database, a reranker, or an LLM judge. This is not a claim that vector retrieval will never be needed. Reconsider the decision when a governed production corpus, a materially broader public corpus, or new failures show that deterministic lexical retrieval cannot meet the same evidence, no-answer, scope-isolation, and grounded-runtime gates.
